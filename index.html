What We Do

Full-Service Diesel & Gas Vehicle Repair

From routine maintenance to high-performance builds — we do it all.

Fleet Maintenance

Preventive maintenance programs for commercial and personal fleets. Oil changes, fuel & air filter services, transmission services, differential services, A/C service, and brake inspections for Ram, Ford, and GM vehicles.

Engine Diagnostics & Repair

Check engine light? We diagnose and repair all codes across intake, exhaust, aftertreatment, evaporative emissions, fuel, and cooling systems.

Heavy Line & Internal Engine

Deep internal engine work including rotating assemblies, valvetrain, cylinder heads & gaskets, timing components, and crankcase ventilation systems.

High Performance Upgrades

Authorized dealer for KC Turbos, Snyder Performance Engineering, Titan Turbo, S&S Diesel, FASS Fuel Systems, S&B Filters, and No Limit Fabrication. We build trucks that perform.

Steering & Suspension

Full steering and suspension repair and modification — tie rod ends, drag links, ball joints, track bars, shocks, springs, control arms, and more for all major brands.

Cooling System Service

Water pumps, thermostats, hoses, radiators, and coolant exchanges to keep your engine temperature in check.

Transmission Diagnostics

Diagnosis and repair of external and most internal transmission control components.

Differential Diagnostics

Ring and pinion replacement, differential bearing replacement, axle and hub bearing service.

A/C System Repair

Full A/C system diagnosis and repair for R-134a and R-1234yf systems — compressors, condensers, expansion valves, driers, and electrical components.
