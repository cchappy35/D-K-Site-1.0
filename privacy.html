Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

This policy explains what information D&K Services Co. ("D&K," "we," "us") collects when you visit dkservicesco.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

When you fill out a service request form, we collect the details you enter: your name, phone number, email address, vehicle information, the service you need, and any message you write. You can also contact us directly by phone or email.

Form submissions are delivered to our business email through a form-processing service (Web3Forms). We use this information only to respond to your request, schedule and perform service, and follow up about your vehicle.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, ours relies on services that receive basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, and the pages you view:

  • Hosting and security — our site is served through Cloudflare, which processes visit data to deliver pages and protect against abuse.
  • Fonts and maps — we load fonts from Google Fonts and show an embedded Google Map of our shop, so Google receives standard request data when those load.
  • Analytics and advertising — we may use tools such as Google Analytics, Google Ads, and Meta (Facebook/Instagram) advertising to understand how visitors use the site and to measure and improve our ads. These tools may use cookies or similar technologies to record things like which page you came from and whether you submitted a form or tapped to call.

How we use information

  • To respond to service requests and questions
  • To schedule, perform, and follow up on vehicle service
  • To understand how the website is used and improve it
  • To measure the results of our advertising
  • To keep the site secure and prevent spam or abuse

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the website and our business (such as the services named above), when required by law, or to protect our rights and the safety of others.

Your choices

  • You can set your browser to block or delete cookies.
  • You can manage how Google uses ad data at adssettings.google.com, and opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on.
  • You can adjust Meta ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram account settings.
  • You can ask us to update or delete information you've submitted by contacting us below.

Data retention and security

We keep service requests and related records as long as needed to serve you and meet our business and legal obligations. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no website or email system is completely secure.

Children

Our website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed.
